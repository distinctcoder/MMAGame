How to block

Hold a direction (W, A, S, D) and press Space. Space alone guards the body. Or tap the pad.

Strike chart

Windup is how long you have to read it. Range is the distance between the fighters' hips where it lands; outside it the strike whiffs and you recover slowly.

Strategy

  • Right block, wrong block. The matching block stops a punch completely and takes most of the sting out of a kick. A high guard still catches half of a hook. Covering the wrong side leaves you open for full damage.
  • Block late for a counter. Raise the block in the last moment before impact to parry. Your next strike comes out faster and hits harder.
  • Checked kicks hurt the kicker. A checked low kick damages the kicker's leg instead.
  • Speed beats power. Landing any strike interrupts the other fighter's windup, so a jab can stop a head kick.
  • Range. Hooks only land close. Kicks need distance. Step in and out to stay where your strikes land and theirs don't.
  • Stamina. Every strike costs stamina. When it runs low, strikes get slower and weaker. Body damage lowers your stamina ceiling.
  • Three ways to lose. Head damage ends in a knockout. Leg damage or body damage ends in a TKO. If the clock runs out, the judges score total damage.
