Fight log

    How the takedown works

    1. Level change. The shooter drops their hips. If the defender reads it (takedown defense beats takedowns by a clear margin), the shot is stuffed before it starts.
    2. The grab. The shooter wraps the legs. A big defender win is a sprawl and both reset on the feet. A small defender win still goes to the mat, but the defender lands in guard.
    3. The finish. A shooter win drives through. The bigger the win, the better the landing: side control, then mount.
    4. On the mat. Top punches and passes. Bottom blocks, sweeps (mount and guard sweeps flip who is on top) and submits from guard. Mount top can attack an arm-triangle.
