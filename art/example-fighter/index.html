Parts sheet

This is everything an artist hands over for this fighter. Each part is drawn once, upright, on a transparent background.

The pivot: the joint the part rotates around. It must sit in the same spot on every version of a part.

Rules for the artist

  • Draw the near side onlyThe far arm and leg reuse the same drawings. The engine shades them darker and draws them behind the body.
  • Overlap at the jointsRound off the ends of each limb so elbows and knees never show a gap when they bend.
  • Same canvas, same pivotGear versions of a part (gloves, shorts) keep the base part's size and pivot so they swap in cleanly.
  • Facing right, 1× scaleThe fighter faces right and stands about 290px tall. Deliver SVG, or PNG at 2×.

Bone lengths, pivots and attach points live in rig.json. Poses and timing live in animations.json. The full spec is in docs/SPRITE_SPEC.md.
